Depicting signs 2: size & shape

The second kind of depicting sign shows what things look like: their size and shape. Your hands trace outlines and show dimensions, so you can describe objects, furniture and rooms without needing a sign for every single thing.

By the end you can…

  • Show size: BIG, SMALL, LONG, THIN, WIDE
  • Trace shapes: SQUARE, round, a box
  • Describe objects and furniture
  • Describe the layout of a room

1 · Size

Both curved hands move apart: showing a big size.

Both hands close together, showing a small size. (Southern starts from the mouth; NSW is near the mouth.)

Both fists pull apart sideways: showing length.

Flat hand moves down in front of the body: describing something thin and tall.

Both flat hands, palms facing, move wide apart.

2 · Shape and objects

Index fingers trace a square in the air.

Southern: index fingers cross in front of the body. Northern: both curved hands shape a ball. SA: hands cupped.

Both flat hands outline the sides, then top and bottom, of a box.

Both flat hands, palms down, sweep apart: a flat surface.

Both hands outline the walls of a room. Watch at 0.5×.
Trace it

To describe an object, trace its outline with index fingers or flat hands: a round table, a long thin road, a square window. Your face adds detail: puffed cheeks for big or thick, pursed lips for thin or small.

Layouts

For a room, set up the space in front of you as the room: ROOM (outline), TABLE there (flat hands), WINDOW there (trace a square). Then you can point to anything in it later. This combines with Lesson 11's signing space.

Deaf culture: Description is everything

Rich visual description is valued in Deaf culture. Describing exactly what something looked like, often in more detail than an English speaker would, is good Auslan, not over-explaining.

Put it together

Mirror practice

Press M for the mirror and R to record a take, then compare it side by side with the reference.

  1. Size with face. BIG with puffed cheeks, SMALL with pursed lips. Exaggerate it!
  2. Trace objects. Describe three objects near you: shape, size, colour.
  3. Your room. Outline your room, then place the bed, table and window in it.
  4. Combine. CAR BIG RED · BOX SMALL SQUARE.
  5. Record a description of your kitchen table and what's on it.

Check yourself

Recap

Size and shape are traced in space, with your face showing how big or small. Set up rooms like maps and point back to things in them.
