New signs

Australia-wide: thumbs-up hand moves down the chest. Tasmania: both open hands at the chest.

One hand comes down onto the back of the other hand.

Two thumbs-up hands meet in front of the chest. Watch at 0.5×.

Both hands at the chest, fingertips meeting. Watch at 0.5×.

Hand at the cheek with a small shaking movement. Watch at 0.5×.

Both hands bent like paws in front of the chest: a begging dog.

Thumb and index pinch at the cheek and pull out: whiskers.

Warm-up: Unit 2 signs

Both hands circle out and come together: a group.

The letter M (three fingers) tapped on the palm.

The letter F tapped twice.

Bent index finger rubs the side of the nose.

Two fists side by side rub against each other.

Flat hand brushes up the other palm repeatedly, with a smile.

Bent hand at the chest drops down; tired face, slumped shoulders.

Flat hand raised high, palm down: showing someone's height.

Pinch a bit of hair at the side of your head.

Point to where the person is, or to the spot you've set up for them.

Index sweeps across the places where those people are.

Two-finger (V) hand moves between the two people.

Model: “my family”

Watch each line, then sign it. Notice how each person gets a spot, and the pointing refers back to it.

Unit 2 can-do check

Record each one (M, then R, length 8–12 s). If you can do them without peeking, you're ready for Unit 3.

  1. Your family: introduce at least three family members, each placed in their own spot.
  2. Describe them: height, hair, glasses/beard, old/young, pointing back to each spot.
  3. Feelings: say how two of them are today, with matching faces (one with VERY).
  4. Questions: YOU HAVE BROTHER? · YOUR FAMILY LIVE WHERE? · YOUR BIRTHDAY WHEN?
  5. Money & numbers: a price with HOW-MUCH? and an answer with DOLLAR.
  6. Compare with your Unit 1 recording. Notice what's smoother now.
