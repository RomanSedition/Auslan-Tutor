Both open hands close and open in front of you, marking the next step. Watch at 0.5×.

Index finger pointing up from beside the little finger of the other hand: 'last one'.

Fist touches the forehead, then holds still. (SA: then both fists together at the chest.)

How to build a signed story

A story recipe
  1. Time: LAST-WEEK, YESTERDAY, SATURDAY…
  2. Place: set it up in space: BEACH, CITY, SHOP…
  3. People: introduce them and give each a spot.
  4. Events: FIRST…, THEN…, THEN…: directional verbs, depicting signs for movement.
  5. Reactions: role shift into the characters: SURPRISED, LAUGH, SHOCKED.
  6. Ending: FINALLY…, and how you felt.

Model story: “The beach”

Final can-do check

Record your own story, 30–60 seconds (use the mirror with the longest recording length, or record it in parts).

  1. Choose something real: a weekend, a trip, a funny moment, a meal out.
  2. Plan it with the recipe above. Jot down the signs you need; look up any you don't know in the Glossary.
  3. Rehearse in the mirror two or three times, focusing on time first, consistent spots and facial expression.
  4. Record your story, then download it.
  5. Compare with your Lesson 6 recording. Notice your fluency, your face and your use of space.
  6. Share it with a Deaf teacher or an Auslan class if you can. Real feedback is the best next step.
