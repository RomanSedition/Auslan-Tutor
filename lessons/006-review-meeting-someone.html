New signs

Hands clasped together in front of the body.

Hand moves away from a starting point, as in 'come from'. Watch at 0.5×.

Hand moves back and forth between you and the other person: 'me too, same'.

Dominant hand touches the second finger of the other hand, like listing a second item.

Thumbs-up hand rocks from side to side.
SAME: “me too!”

SAME is a natural reaction when you find something in common: YOU LEARN AUSLAN? SAME!. It's directed between you and the other person.

Unit 1 can-do check

Record each of these in the mirror (M, then R; set the length to 8–12 s). If you can do them without peeking, you're ready for Unit 2.

  1. Introduce yourself: greeting, name (fingerspelled smoothly), where you're from, that you're hearing and learning Auslan.
  2. Ask three questions with the right face: YOUR NAME WHAT? · YOU FROM WHERE? · YOU DEAF?
  3. Numbers: your age and your phone number, digit by digit.
  4. Rescue yourself: AGAIN PLEASE · SLOW PLEASE · ME UNDERSTAND (headshake) · FINGERSPELL PLEASE.
  5. Close the conversation: NICE MEET YOU · THANK-YOU · SEE-YOU-AGAIN · BYE.
  6. Compare. Download this take and keep it. You'll compare it with your Lesson 30 story.
