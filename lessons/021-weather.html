Weather & seasons

Everyone talks about the weather. These signs are wonderfully visual (rain falls, the sun bursts open, wind sways), and they're great practice for using your face to show intensity.

By the end you can…

  • Sign weather words and the four seasons
  • Show intensity: a bit hot versus scorching
  • Talk about the weekend weather
  • Ask about the weather

1 · Weather

Both open hands raised, palms out, wiggling.

Hand wipes from the mouth/chin outward, as if fanning heat away.

Both fists shiver in front of the body. Also means WINTER.

Bent fingers move down in front of the body: rain falling.

Fist above the head springs open: sun rays.

Both open hands sway from side to side: wind blowing.

Index finger zigzags down from above: lightning.

Curved hands circle overhead: clouds rolling across the sky.

2 · Seasons

Index finger wipes across the forehead: wiping off sweat.

Both hands, fingers spread, flutter down: leaves falling. Also means COOL.

Both fists shiver in front of the body. Also means WINTER.

Hand at the cheek, fingers opening. Signbank lists this one without a region. Watch at 0.5×.

WINTER and COLD share a sign. AUTUMN also means COOL.

Size the weather

Weather signs change with intensity: light RAIN is small, gentle finger movement; a downpour is fast, heavy and wide, with a matching face. Same for WIND (breeze versus gale) and HOT.

Put it together

Mirror practice

Press M for the mirror and R to record a take, then compare it side by side with the reference.

  1. Weather words. All eight, then all four seasons.
  2. Intensity. RAIN three ways: drizzle, rain, storm. Then HOT three ways.
  3. Forecast. Sign a weather forecast: TODAY …, TOMORROW …, SATURDAY …
  4. Opinions. Which season do you like? Which don't you like (headshake)?
  5. Record your forecast like a TV weather presenter.

Check yourself

Recap

Weather signs are visual, and their size and speed show intensity. COLD = WINTER; AUTUMN = COOL.
