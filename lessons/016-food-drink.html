Food & drink

Order a coffee, say what you like and don't like, and say when you're hungry. HUNGRY alone has four regional versions, including one from WA.

By the end you can…

  • Sign common food and drinks
  • Say HUNGRY and THIRSTY in your region's version
  • Say what you LIKE and WANT
  • Order at a café

Click a video to play. Use 0.25×/0.5× to slow it down. 🪞 Practise puts a sign beside your mirror. Signs with Regional versions show your state's version first.

1 · Drinks

Thumb and fingers in a C, tapping at the cheek.

Fingertips together, small circular stirring above the other hand, like stirring a cuppa.

Southern: thumb and index tap at the chin, like the letter W. Northern: flat hand at the mouth.

Australia-wide: both fists squeeze alternately, like milking. NSW: hand near the mouth.

2 · Food

Flat hand slices across the other palm: cutting bread.

Australia-wide: pinch the fleshy part of the other hand. Southern: index at the chin. Northern: from the cheek.

Hand circles at the cheek, as if holding a round fruit, then to the chin.

Hand at the chin with a small repeated movement. Watch at 0.5×.

3 · Wanting and liking

Four regional versions! Southern: thumbs-up hand at the stomach/chest. WA: open hand at the face closes. Northern: both hands on the stomach.

Index finger traces down the throat.

Flat hand brushes up the chest.

Australia-wide: flat hand on the chest moves out, palm up. SA: palm-up hand pulls towards you.
Don't like, don't want

Add a headshake to LIKE or WANT: ME LIKE COFFEE + headshake = I don't like coffee. You'll learn more negative signs next lesson.

Deaf culture: Ordering when you're Deaf

Many Deaf people order by pointing at the menu, writing it down, or typing on a phone. If a Deaf customer is ordering from you, any of these are fine; just keep eye contact and be patient. Some cafés are learning a few signs, and COFFEE is usually the first!

Put it together

Mirror practice

Press M for the mirror and R to record a take, then compare it side by side with the reference.

  1. Café run. Order: COFFEE MILK PLEASE → HOW-MUCH? → FIVE DOLLAR → THANK-YOU.
  2. Likes. Three things you LIKE (nod) and three you don't (headshake).
  3. Regional HUNGRY. Click through all four versions and sign each one.
  4. Ask. YOU HUNGRY? YOU THIRSTY? YOU WANT WHAT?, each with the right face.
  5. Record a café conversation, both sides.
