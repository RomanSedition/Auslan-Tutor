Model conversation: a day at the beach

Unit 4 can-do check

  1. Invite: invite someone somewhere, with day and time.
  2. Directions: explain how to get from your house to a place, using space.
  3. Transport: say how you'll get there and whether it's near or far.
  4. Weather: give the forecast for the day.
  5. Activities: say what you'll do there and what you like or prefer.
  6. Compare with your Unit 3 recording.
