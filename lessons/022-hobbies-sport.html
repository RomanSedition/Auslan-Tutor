Hobbies & sport

Talk about what you do for fun, which is the heart of a lot of conversations. You'll also learn FAVOURITE and PREFER for comparing.

By the end you can…

  • Sign common hobbies and sports
  • Ask what someone likes doing
  • Say your favourite and what you prefer
  • Combine with time: WEEKEND ME SWIM

1 · Sport

Thumbs-up hands with a small alternating movement.

Arms move as if swimming freestyle.

Hand kicks into the other: kicking a ball.

Both hands hold an imaginary bat and swing.

Both open hands shake loosely at the sides.

Thumbs-up fists move against each other: competing.

2 · Hobbies

V-hand (eyes) moves down across the other palm (a page).

Australia-wide: open hand flickers against the other palm: a flickering screen. SA: both hands flicker.

Index fingers wave in time, like conducting. (Signbank uses this same video for one Queensland BIRTHDAY.)

Fist stirs or flips over the other hand, like stirring a pot.

Both index fingers point forward and move away together.

3 · Preferences

Thumb taps at the chin, while the other hand's index is up. Watch at 0.5×.

Flat hand brushes off the other palm, choosing one over the other.
Deaf culture: Deaf sport

Deaf sport is a big part of the community. There are Deaf sporting clubs and the Deaflympics, international games held every four years since 1924. Signals are visual: flags and lights instead of whistles and starting guns.

Deaf culture: Movies and music

Captions make movies accessible, and many Deaf people campaign for captioning in cinemas and online. Some Deaf people enjoy music through vibration, visuals, or sign-song performances; others aren't interested in it. Both are completely normal.

Put it together

Mirror practice

Press M for the mirror and R to record a take, then compare it side by side with the reference.

  1. Hobbies. Sign all of them, then pick your top three.
  2. Favourite. MY FAVOURITE … (sport, movie, food).
  3. Prefer. Pick pairs: COFFEE TEA → ME PREFER … ; MOVIE READ → …
  4. Ask. YOU LIKE WHAT? · YOUR FAVOURITE WHAT? with eyebrows down.
  5. Record yourself introducing your hobbies (10 s).

Check yourself

Recap

Hobbies + LIKE / FAVOURITE / PREFER = lots of conversation. Ask with eyebrows down and compare with WHICH.
