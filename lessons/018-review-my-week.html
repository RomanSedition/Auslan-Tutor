Warm-up: Unit 3 signs

Australia-wide: index finger held up, small downward movement (also means NOW). Northern: flat hands at the chest.

Index finger at the cheek, moving forward: the future is in front of you.

Southern: index finger at the cheek/chin, moving back over the shoulder. Northern: hand at the chin moves back.

Open hands, palms facing you, flip out and down: 'done, finished'. Also marks the past.

Bent fingers (a claw) at the chin, moving forward.

Both hands with crossed fingers near the chest, moving down. Watch at 0.5×.

Index finger taps the back of the other wrist: where a watch goes.

Flat hand taps the edge of the other hand repeatedly.

Bunched fingertips move to the mouth: putting food in.

Thumb and fingers close in front of the eye: eyes closing.

Thumb and fingers in a C, tapping at the cheek.

Four regional versions! Southern: thumbs-up hand at the stomach/chest. WA: open hand at the face closes. Northern: both hands on the stomach.

Model conversation: making plans

Unit 3 can-do check

  1. Your day: morning to night, in order, using time words first.
  2. Your week: one thing for each day, Monday to Sunday.
  3. Yesterday: three things you did, with FINISH.
  4. Food: order at a café and say one thing you don't like (headshake).
  5. Plans: invite someone (YOU FREE?) and decline politely (ME BUSY / ME CAN'T).
  6. Compare with your Unit 2 recording.
