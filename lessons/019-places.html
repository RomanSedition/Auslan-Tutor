Places around town

Unit 4 takes you out of the house. Learn places around town, and use signing space to set them up and point back to them, just like you did with people in Lesson 11.

By the end you can…

  • Sign common places
  • Set up places in space and point back to them
  • Ask and answer WHERE questions about places
  • Ask for the toilet!

Shared signs

A few of these share a video with other signs on Signbank: the Australia-wide TOILET is the letter T (the same as TUESDAY), and the Southern BEACH is the same as the Northern YESTERDAY. Context makes it clear.

How it works

Places in space

Just like people, places get spots. SHOP (point left), SCHOOL (point right). Then ME GO (towards the left) means 'I'm going to the shop'. The real direction doesn't matter unless you're giving real directions (next lesson).

Deaf culture: Deaf schools

Deaf schools have been central to Deaf community life in Australia, and many regional signs trace back to the different Deaf schools in each state. Many older Deaf people met their lifelong friends at Deaf school.

Put it together

Mirror practice

Press M for the mirror and R to record a take, then compare it side by side with the reference.

  1. Places. All nine, twice through.
  2. Set up a map. Place HOUSE (centre), SHOP (left), BEACH (right), SCHOOL (front). Point to each.
  3. Go places. Use your map: ME GO SHOP (towards the left), THEN BEACH (towards the right).
  4. Ask. TOILET WHERE? · YOU LIVE WHERE? with the wh-face.
  5. Record your neighbourhood: what's near your house.

Recap

Places get spots in space, just like people. GO moves towards them. Some signs share a form with others; context does the work.
