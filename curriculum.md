# Auslan curriculum (beginner track)

A beginner track. Claude follows this order for "next lesson", adapts it to what's asked for,
and ticks items off as lessons are written.
Each lesson should reuse 3–5 signs from earlier lessons in its phrases and quiz, as spaced review.

## Unit 1: Foundations
- [x] 1. Fingerspelling: the two-handed alphabet (vowels, palm letters, name spelling)
- [x] 2. Greetings & introductions (HELLO, HOW ARE YOU, NAME, WHAT, NICE MEET, DEAF/HEARING, LEARN AUSLAN; eyebrow grammar)
- [ ] 3. Numbers 1–20 (and age: ME AGE 30)
- [ ] 4. Questions & facial expression (WHO, WHERE, WHEN, WHY, HOW, YES, NO; yes/no vs wh- faces in depth)
- [ ] 5. Family (MOTHER, FATHER, SISTER, BROTHER, PARTNER, CHILD; HAVE / NOT-HAVE)
- [ ] 6. Feelings (HAPPY, SAD, TIRED, ANGRY, EXCITED, SICK; intensity through the face)

## Unit 2: Everyday life
- [ ] 7. Everyday verbs (EAT, DRINK, SLEEP, WORK, GO, COME, LIKE, WANT, KNOW, UNDERSTAND)
- [ ] 8. Time & days (TODAY, TOMORROW, YESTERDAY, days of the week; time comes first in the sentence)
- [ ] 9. Food & drink (COFFEE, TEA, WATER, BREAD...; ordering at a café)
- [ ] 10. Places & directions (HOME, SHOP, WORK, SCHOOL; pointing and space)
- [ ] 11. Colours & describing things
- [ ] 12. Review & conversation: a mixed review lesson for Unit 1–2

## Unit 3: Grammar in space
- [ ] 13. Using signing space: setting up people and places, then pointing back to them
- [ ] 14. Directional verbs (GIVE, ASK, TELL, HELP: movement shows who does what to whom)
- [ ] 15. Negation & “not yet” (headshake, NOT, NOTHING, NEVER)
- [ ] 16. Classifiers intro (depicting vehicles, people and objects)

## Communication strategies (any time)
- Asking someone to repeat or sign slower, saying “I don't understand”
- Clarifying through fingerspelling
